- Artist profiles with user_id NULL are admin-created (manual); their photos live under artist-photos/manual/, writable only by admins. Why: lets admins add artists without fake accounts while owner RLS stays untouched.
- All text sizing flows from the `--type-scale` variable in `src/styles.css` (Tailwind `text-*` tokens and the `.btn`/`.sticker`/`.vertical-text` font sizes are computed from it). To make text bigger or smaller site-wide, change that one variable; never hardcode per-component font sizes or px letter sizes. Why: the user wants a single "zoom" control over letter size across the whole site.
- Keep the equipment offer in the shared EquipmentSection used by Home and Services, with each modality linking to the existing general contact form. Why: avoids duplicated offer markup and separate booking logic.
- Booking uses one approved-artist dataset for map and list, with Leaflet loaded only after hydration and GeoNames city centers bundled locally; unknown or ambiguous cities stay unlocated. Why: preserves privacy, avoids per-visitor geocoding and keeps city filtering synchronized without schema changes.
- Public legal documents share a presentation-only LegalPage and PrivacyNotice; notices do not gate forms or imply recorded acceptance. Why: legal information remains accessible without changing authentication or submission behavior.
